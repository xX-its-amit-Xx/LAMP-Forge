Why this exists

Loop-mediated Isothermal Amplification (LAMP, Notomi 2000) is the workhorse of point-of-care molecular diagnostics — it runs at a single temperature, reads out by eye, and works in field conditions PCR can't reach. But it needs six primers binding to eight regions, each thermodynamically clean and specific. Designing that set by hand is tedious; the few commercial tools that exist are closed-source and opaque.

LAMP-Forge gives you the whole pipeline in one open container, with every design decision documented in the source. Built so a wet-lab scientist with intermediate Python can audit the trade-offs.
